Choose the answers that sound most like your way of thinking. At the end, you will see which classic sociologist your answers most closely match. This is not a scientific personality test; it is a playful way to learn major sociological ideas.

How this works

Each answer adds points to one or more sociologists. Your top result shows the thinker whose ideas most closely match your responses. Your second result shows another lens you may also use when thinking about society.

Instructor note: This activity introduces major thinkers often included in Introduction to Sociology: Marx, Durkheim, Weber, Martineau, Du Bois, Addams, Mead, and Goffman.
