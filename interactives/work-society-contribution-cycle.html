What Does the Model Reveal?

Durkheim: Specialized roles can create organic solidarity because people depend on one another.

Critical lens: Having a job is not enough if pay, safety, dignity, or access remain unequal.

Ideal Society Question: What would your society change first, and who might still be left out?

Model limits: These relationships are simplified for classroom exploration. Real outcomes also depend on history, discrimination, technology, geography, policy design, family circumstances, disability, global markets, and many other factors. Correlation does not prove that employment alone caused an outcome.
