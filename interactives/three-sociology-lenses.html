2. Click the Theory Cards

Each theory asks a different kind of question. Click each card to reveal its main question, focus, and common clue words.

⚙️

Structural Functionalism

Big idea: Society is a system made of connected parts.

Main question: How does this help society function or stay stable?

Focus: order, stability, institutions, functions, roles, cooperation.

Watch for: “What purpose does it serve?”

⚔️

Conflict Theory

Big idea: Society involves inequality and competition.

Main question: Who benefits, who is harmed, and who has power?

Focus: inequality, resources, privilege, oppression, competition, change.

Watch for: “Who gets more and who gets less?”

💬

Symbolic Interactionism

Big idea: Society is created through everyday meanings.

Main question: What meanings do people create through interaction?

Focus: symbols, language, labels, identity, communication, interpretation.

Watch for: “What does this mean to people?”
