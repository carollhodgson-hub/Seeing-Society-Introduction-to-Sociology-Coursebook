UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP first. Upload the extracted files and the entire images/ and interactives/ folders, keeping their names and structure.
2. index.html must be in the repository root, beside book.css, book.js, key-concepts.js, and the two folders. Do not upload just the ZIP, and do not flatten the images folder.
3. Include .nojekyll (an empty configuration file). This prevents Jekyll from processing this plain HTML website.
4. In repository Settings > Pages, select Deploy from a branch, choose main and /(root), and save. Wait until the deployment succeeds.
5. Open the actual Pages URL. Check the home page, every chapter, glossary search, and the interactive activities before replacing any Canvas links.
