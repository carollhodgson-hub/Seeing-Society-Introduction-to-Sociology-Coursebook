Family, memory, and method

A story helps us remember; evidence helps us know

Eleanor’s family gives abstract concepts a human scale. The narrative illustrates possibilities, but it is not a dataset and cannot prove that every family experienced an era in the same way. Use the story to form questions, then use historical sources and sociological research to test explanations.
